## Question and answer

State the research question, relevant scope, concise answer, and confidence with an evidential justification. An unresolved or negative result is acceptable. Distinguish sourced findings, reproduced results, inferences, and assumptions. For mathematical claims, give the precise statement and assumptions, identify conjectures or remaining proof obligations, and state the [mathematical support obtained](../AGENTS.md#mathematical-support).

## Key evidence and quantitative results

Explain the evidence that determines the answer. Link important claims to evidence records and precise original sources; for example, a record link from this directory starts with `../evidence/RECORDS.md`. Include applicable methods, units, uncertainty, sensitivity, and calculation/artifact links so the results can be checked.

## Alternatives and strongest counterevidence

Describe consequential competing explanations, why any were rejected, what remains viable, and the strongest evidence against the leading interpretation. Preserve disagreement between sources and explain its relevance. For conjectures, summarize counterexample-search coverage and limits, linking records rather than repeating search logs.

## Uncertainty and limitations

Identify remaining unknowns, consequential assumptions, access or verification gaps, limitations of data and methods, and the conditions under which the answer may not hold.

## Implications and next evidence

Explain what the result implies for the objective. Where uncertainty remains, specify the next discriminating evidence or experiment, expected outcomes under surviving explanations, and how the conclusion would change. State why investigation stopped, whether the objective was resolved or a limit reached, and what would justify resuming.

## Source and artifact provenance

Provide the essential source citations and analysis/data references with precise locators or links to records containing them. This may be integrated into earlier sections when a separate list would duplicate them. Identify evidence coverage and distinguish source-reported results from independent checks.
